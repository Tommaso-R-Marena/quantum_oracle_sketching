\section{Interferometric Classical Shadow}
\label{sec:shadow}

We implement and validate the interferometric classical shadow protocol of Chen et al.~\cite{chen2023}.  Given a reference state $w\in\C^n$ and a test observable $O = |v\rangle\langle v|$, the shadow estimator for $\langle v|\rho|v\rangle$ via $T$ random interferometric measurements satisfies
\[
  \E[\hat{O}_T] = \langle v|\rho|v\rangle, \qquad \mathrm{Var}[\hat{O}_T] = O(s/T),
\]
where $s = |\supp(v)|$ is the sparsity of the test vector.  Our open-source simulation is, to our knowledge, the first publicly available implementation of this protocol.  Empirical results in the companion notebooks confirm the $O(\sqrt{s/T})$ error scaling.

Let $\mathrm{bit} \sim \mathrm{Bernoulli}\!\left(\tfrac{1}{2}(1 - \mathrm{Re}\langle w|U_w\rangle)\right)$ where $U_w$ applies random phases to a permuted copy of $w$.  Then the corrected estimator $Z = (1-2\,\mathrm{bit})\cdot \mathrm{Re}\langle U_w|x\rangle$ is unbiased: $\E[Z] = \mathrm{Re}\langle w|x\rangle$, with $\mathrm{Var}[Z_T] \leq 2/T$.
