\section{Replication of Zhao et al.\ Scaling Laws}
\label{sec:core}

We replicate the six-dataset benchmark suite of \Zhao\ using the companion notebook \texttt{real\_datasets\_colab.ipynb} and the committed publication bundle (Section~\ref{sec:experiments}).  Each dataset emits machine-size vs.\ accuracy curves and variance-recovery diagnostics for four construction methods: quantum oracle sketching, QRAM-based, classical sparse, and classical streaming.

\subsection{Datasets and Experimental Setup}

Per-dataset regularization (\texttt{CLF\_ALPHA\_PER\_DATASET}): IMDb $\alpha=10$, 20 Newsgroups $\alpha=1$, PBMC3k/PBMC68k $\alpha=200$, Dorothea $\alpha=200$, Splice $\alpha=10$.  PBMC68k uses the full $\sim$68k-cell download via \texttt{scvelo} (cached by \texttt{load\_pbmc68k()}).  Full dataset metadata appear in Appendix~\ref{app:datasets}.
