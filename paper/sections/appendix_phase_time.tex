\section{Phase Time Lower Bounds for Expected-Unitary Oracles}
\label{app:phase}

For the expected-unitary oracle sketch (Equation~\eqref{eq:logsum}), the phase time $t$ controls the per-entry phase increment.  A natural conjecture is that $t \geq \Omega(N/K)$ is required for $\eps$-accurate support reconstruction.  Formal analysis in the planned \texttt{PhaseTimeBound.lean} artifact shows this conjecture is \emph{false} in general: concentrated importance distributions can achieve zero off-support error with $t \in [K\pi(1-\eps/2),\, \pi(K + \eps N/2)]$.

Tighter $N/K$ scaling can hold under additional constraints: (a)~a blind-oracle query model, (b)~a minimum probability floor $p(x) \geq c/N$, or (c)~circuit-depth budgets that forbid extreme concentration.  The executable implementation in \texttt{src/qos/theory/} and tests in \texttt{tests/test\_theory\_fixes.py} pin the numerically verified phase-time envelopes used in the benchmark drivers.
