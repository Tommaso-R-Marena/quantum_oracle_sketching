\title{Adaptive Quantum Oracle Sketching with Sublinear Sample Complexity}

\author{Tommaso R. Marena}
\affiliation{Department of Mathematics and Statistics, The Catholic University of America, Washington, DC 20064}
\email{marena@cua.edu}

\date{\today}

\begin{abstract}
We present three improvements to the quantum oracle sketching framework of Zhao et al.\ (2026), which constructs a phase-oracle diagonal at cost $O(NQ^2)$ for domain size $N$ and $Q$ linear queries.  For $K$-sparse Boolean functions and Fourier-sparse signals with $K_F$ dominant modes, this cost is not necessary.  (1)~Adaptive importance sampling reduces cost to $O(KQ^2)$, improving by $N/K$ with matching lower bounds.  (2)~$k$-level hierarchical composition yields $O(NQ^{2-1/k})$ query dependence.  (3)~Variational warmstart lowers effective dimension to $K_F$.  Combined, samples scale as $O(K_F Q^{2-1/k})$, a $76{,}000$-fold reduction at $N=2^{20}$, $K_F=64$, $Q=100$, $k=3$.  Open-source JAX implementations, proofs, and companion Colab notebooks validate all four contributions.
\end{abstract}
